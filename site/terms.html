Your hardware

DispCtrl changes real hardware settings: monitor controls over DDC/CI, display modes, gamma and power. It only offers values a monitor reports it accepts, and never writes an undocumented code unless a reviewed definition marks it safe. Even so, monitors differ, and you use it at your own risk. A monitor's own menu, or DispCtrl's per-display reset, puts things back.
